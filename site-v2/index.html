Questions

Straight answers.

Something we didn’t cover? Email hello@gmwebco.com and we’ll reply personally.

How much does a website cost?

Websites start at $250 for a Starter site, $300 for Business and $500 for Premium. Every project is different, so we confirm a clear, fixed quote on your free call before any work begins.

How long does it take?

Most websites are live in about a week. Larger sites or custom features can take longer, and we’ll give you a clear timeline on the call.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your name, and your text, photos and site files are yours. If you ever want to move on, we hand everything over.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We keep what already works, like your content, branding and domain, and rebuild the rest so the site looks established and is easy to use on a phone.

Do you provide hosting?

Yes. Our optional care plan is $49 a month and covers hosting, fixes and updates. If you’d rather host elsewhere, we’ll set the site up so you can.

Can you connect my domain?

Yes. We connect your existing domain, or help you register a new one in your name.

Can you add online booking?

Yes. We can add appointment booking or quote-request forms so customers can take the next step right from your website.

Will my website work on mobile?

Yes. Every site is designed phone-first and tested on real screen sizes, because most of your customers will see it on a phone.

Can you help with Google and SEO?

Yes. Every site includes SEO basics: page titles, descriptions, clean structure and fast loading. Business and Premium sites are also connected to your Google Business profile.

What happens after my website launches?

We check that everything works, hand over your site, and stay a message away. If you want ongoing updates, the optional care plan covers them.
