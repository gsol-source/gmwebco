How long does a website take?+

Most sites are done in about a week. Custom features can take a little longer. We’ll give you a clear timeline on the call.

Do I need my text and photos ready?+

No. We can write your text with you and help pick or source photos. Bring what you have, and we’ll work out the rest together.

Who owns the website?+

You do. The domain is registered in your name, and your text and photos are yours. If you ever move on, we hand everything over.

Is there a monthly commitment?+

No. The $49/month care plan is optional. It covers hosting, updates, and fixes. If you’d rather manage hosting yourself, we can help you get set up.

How does payment work?+

Half when we start and half when your site goes live. We agree on the price before any work begins, so there are no surprises.

Can I update the site after launch?+

Absolutely. On the care plan, send us your new hours, photos, prices, or other changes and we’ll handle them. We can discuss other arrangements on the call too.
