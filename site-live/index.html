FAQ

Good questions

How long does a website take?

Most sites are done in under a week. If you need more specific or custom features, it can take a little longer — we’ll give you a clear timeline on our call.

Do I need my content ready?

No. We can help write your text and pick photos.

Can I update the site myself?

You don’t have to — on a care plan, just text us what you want changed and we’ll update it.

Who owns my website?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and your text and photos are yours. If you ever want to move on, we’ll hand everything over.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. The $49/month care plan is optional and covers hosting, updates and fixes. If you’d rather not, we’ll set your site up so you can host it yourself.

How does payment work?

Half when we start and half when your site goes live. We agree on the fixed price before any work begins, so there are no surprise fees.

Can you work with my budget?

Tell us what you can spend on the call. We’ll recommend the package that fits, or suggest what to do first and add the rest later.
